UNDER THE DECK

A practical system for
onboard records.

Quartermaster Aboard is a web interface and Signal K Server plugin running on an onboard Raspberry Pi. Its job is to organize vessel documentation and equipment history where the boat is.

THE CURRENT DESIGN

Simple pieces. Clear roles.

▣

Phone or computer

Open the Quartermaster pages on the boat's network. Scan a QR label to reach an equipment record.

→
Q·

Onboard Raspberry Pi

Signal K Server hosts the Quartermaster plugin and its web pages.

→
▤

Local records

SQLite stores structured records. Photos and documents are kept in the plugin's onboard storage.

The public Netlify website presents the product. It does not store or serve a vessel's Quartermaster database.

WHAT THE SYSTEM HANDLES

Records connected to the asset.

Each equipment record brings its supporting information together so it can be found in context.

01 /

Vessel & equipment

Identify an asset by name, category, maker, model, serial number, location, status, and notes as available.

02 /

Photos & documents

Attach equipment photos and PDF manuals to the corresponding record.

03 /

Parts & maintenance

Keep related parts, service records, operating hours, and maintenance schedules with the equipment.

04 /

QR labels

Assign a unique asset code and print a label whose QR link opens the asset page.

05 /

Photo intake

Capture equipment and nameplate photos for review. AI assisted identification can suggest details for a person to approve.

06 /

Backup & recovery

Keep separate backups of the onboard records and uploaded files as part of the installation procedure.

SCOPE

Focused on documentation.

Quartermaster is a vessel and equipment record system. It is not a navigation display, NMEA gateway, or live instrumentation dashboard. Internet access is not the basis of the onboard record workflow.
