THE VALUE OF A GOOD RECORD

Answers should be
easy to find aboard.

Knowledge about a boat often lives in scattered binders, photo libraries, receipts, and memory. Quartermaster gives each piece of equipment a home for its practical details.

01

Find the right manual

Keep documents with the equipment they describe, ready when you are working on board.

02

See what was done

Record service work and schedules so maintenance history has useful context.

03

Share a clearer picture

Organized records help an owner, technician, surveyor, or future buyer understand the boat.

FROM WALKTHROUGH TO WORKING RECORD

Document once. Find it when it counts.

A straightforward process turns equipment observations into usable onboard records.

1

Inventory

Record the equipment name, location, maker, model, and other useful details.

2

Add context

Attach photos and manuals. Keep parts and service information with the asset.

3

Label

Print a unique QR label for the equipment and place it where it can be scanned.

4

Use & update

Scan from a phone on the boat to open the record and keep it current.
